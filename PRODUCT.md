# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Two audiences, one platform (system name: **Merlin**; public brand: **888 Safety & Training**, a UK fire safety consultancy run by Matt).

- **Clients** (primary): facility and business owners logging in occasionally, usually from a laptop in a daytime office, to fetch compliance documentation, assessment reports, and check their retained consulting hours. State of mind: task-focused, mildly anxious about compliance, reassured by evident professionalism.
- **Admin** (Matt / operators): daily heavy use of the admin console to manage clients, upload documents, track expiries, generate reports.

The login page is client-first: the client form is the primary surface; admin access is a small, unassuming link.

## Product Purpose

A compliance portal for a premium fire safety consultancy. Success means clients find their documents fast and feel they're paying for a high-end professional service, and Matt runs his whole operation from the admin console.

## Brand Personality

Premium consultancy. Calm, editorial, trustworthy. Quietly confident, like a well-set letterhead: serif display type (Newsreader), mono micro-labels, warm cream paper, black ink. Not playful, not techy.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS login templates (gradient blobs, social login buttons, "Welcome back 👋").
- Corporate/government compliance aesthetics: grey, dense, bureaucratic council-website energy.
- Dark techy developer-tool dashboards (neon on black).

## Design Principles

- Paper, not screen: warm cream surfaces, hairline borders, ink-black type. The product should feel like well-produced documentation.
- Editorial hierarchy: Newsreader serif for statements, Inter for UI, JetBrains Mono uppercase micro-labels for wayfinding.
- Client surface is calm and light; admin is the operator cockpit. Never let the admin aesthetic leak into client-facing surfaces.
- Strictly restrained color: cream, ink, hairlines, with brand teal #3b8273 / earth amber only as small functional accents. No drenched or committed color surfaces — a full-teal login concept was explicitly rejected (2026-07).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text; visible focus states; reduced-motion respected (existing global transition gating). No known specialized user needs.
